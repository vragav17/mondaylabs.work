Receive files from Slack, WhatsApp, or Email — and route them automatically to the storage destination of your choice. No manual downloads, no switching apps, zero friction.

  • ☁️ Route to Amazon S3, OneDrive, Google Drive, Azure Blob, or your own custom server

  • 🔌 Bring your own storage — connect any S3-compatible or custom endpoint
